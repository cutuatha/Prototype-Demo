Find your truck

Search current marketplace listings, compare options, and explicitly confirm a truck for planning. Nothing is purchased or financed here.

Core Rules & Decision Ledger
Important: The search buttons open live marketplace pages. This static demo cannot reliably import or refresh a marketplace's listings directly inside this page, so listings you find must be entered below. Do not treat a saved entry as verified unless you checked the original seller listing.

1. Search live truck listings

Filters build a search phrase for marketplace pages. You can change the make, model, sleeper preference, price range, and search area.

Marketplaces control their own results, filters, availability, and listing details. Verify price, mileage, condition, seller, location, and listing status on the source page.

2. Add a listing to compare

Open a marketplace listing, then enter its details here. IDs are assigned by Prototype; the source URL keeps the original listing handy.

3. Compare and select

Use “Compare to Others” to mark options for comparison. “Select Now” only asks you to confirm which truck to carry forward as selected for planning. It does not authorize purchase, financing, funds commitment, or acquisition.
